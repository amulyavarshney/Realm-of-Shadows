# Privacy Policy — Veilbound (MVP)

**Last updated:** 2026-07-16

Veilbound is a local multiplayer game. For the MVP:

## Data we do not collect
- No accounts or email
- No analytics SDKs in MVP
- No advertising identifiers sold to third parties
- No cloud game history

## Data stored on your device
- Display name and avatar choice
- App preferences (sound, haptics, mock mode)
- Temporary room recovery metadata

## Network
Gameplay traffic is sent between devices on your local Wi‑Fi network when you create or join a room. We do not operate a central game server in the MVP.

## Camera
Camera access is used only to scan join QR codes, if you choose that feature.

## Contact
For privacy questions about this project build, contact the app publisher listed on Google Play.

Host this document at a public HTTPS URL before Play production launch.
